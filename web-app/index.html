Game Rules

1. Tap on a card to turn it over.

2. Then, tap another card to find its match.

3. If both cards match, you get a point and an extra turn.

4. If the cards don't match, your turn ends AND the cards swap positions.

5. Try to remember what your cards were.

6. The game finishes once all matching pairs have been found.

7. The player with the highest score wins and equal scores result in a draw.

8. If you have no access to a mouse, WASD keys can be used to navigate the board and the enter key can be used to flip cards. The yellow highlight indicates the currently selected card with this function, which is only activated when WASD key is clicked.
